About PhotoGram

PhotoGram is an admin-curated photo gallery. Visitors can browse freely without accounts. Only the protected Admin Panel can upload, edit or remove photos.

Privacy Policy

PhotoGram does not require visitor accounts. Like preferences are stored locally on the visitor device. Anonymous usage statistics may be stored locally by this demo. No visitor profile is created.
